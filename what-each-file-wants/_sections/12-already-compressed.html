{{S}}Already compressed: leave it

A JPEG, an MP4 or a .zip has already been through an entropy coder. Wrapping eleven such files in four general-purpose codecs found at most 4.66%; re-doing the inner coding found up to 41%. measured here

Figure {{fig-recompress}} wraps eleven files in gzip -9, zstd 19, xz -9 and brotli 11. Five grew under all four, by 5 to 258 bytes; both MP4s changed by at most 0.069%. measured here A tar of five media files, 10,014,720 bytes, shrank 0.441% under zstd 19. measured here That is 44,131 bytes, less than the 45,345 that the five give up one by one (38,008) plus the tar's headers and padding (7,337): side by side, they share nothing zstd could use. reconstruction
