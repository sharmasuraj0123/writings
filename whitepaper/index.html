quirq

A unit of work for intelligence.

The token meters what AI consumes. Nothing meters what it delivers. The quirq is the missing output meter: value budgeted by a human owner, minted by machine verification against captured world state, costed all-in from inference to intervention, and read over time as a trajectory.

01One meter is missing

Every consequential technology gets two meters. Electricity has the watt, and the ledger of what the power produced. Labor has the hour, and the deliverable. AI has one.

Token / the input meter

what the machine drew
  • Counts compute. The atomic events of inference, nearly linear in FLOPs.
  • Scales with energy. And therefore with carbon: real physical accounting.
  • Prices capacity. Infrastructure can plan against it.

A genuinely good unit, for exactly half the question.

Quirq / the output meter

what the machine delivered
  • Outcomes, verified. Scored against a definition of done, from captured state.
  • Budget-denominated. Valued by the human who pays for the outcome.
  • Minted, never self-reported. No component trusts the worker's testimony.

The number a business has been missing.

Divide one by the other and every number an operator needs falls out: cost per quirq quirqs per dollar quirqs per kWh and, tracked over time, the AI effectiveness trajectory of a company.

02The mint

Tokens go in as white light.
Verified value comes out as spectrum.

A quirq is never claimed, it is minted. The environment that hosts the work snapshots the world before execution and after, scores completion V against a weighted definition of done, and mints V · B quirqs from what verifiably changed. The environment is the prism: nothing passes through unrefracted.

Tokens in · what the machine consumes Quirqs out · what the work delivered
1 · Define

The outcome

A machine-checkable definition of done: weighted predicates over world state, stated before execution.

2 · Budget

The value B

The owner prices the outcome: the amount they would pay for it to exist. The task carries B potential quirqs.

3 · Execute

In a workspace

The agent works inside an environment with runtime, memory, files, tools. Every token and API call is metered as a side effect.

4 · Verify

Against state

Snapshot before, snapshot after. Completion V scored from the world itself, never the agent's account of what it did.

5 · Settle

Mint V · B

Verification mints V · B quirqs of delivered work into a tamper-evident ledger, alongside everything production consumed.

07Gaming the quirq

A unit of account is a target.
This one is built to fail loudly.

Quirq accounting does not escape Goodhart's law; it is engineered so that gaming leaves marks where activity metrics fail silently. The attack surface, in order of severity:

AttackIf quirqs are the KPI, inflate B.

DefenseThe budget is set by the party paying it, so inflation is self-taxing wherever budgets clear against real money. Notional budgets are benchmarked against historical human cost, market rates, and sampled value audits. The ledger makes inflation visible as a drifting ratio of budget to audited value.

AttackManufacture units whose checks are trivially green.

DefenseGold-check sampling on a random audit set, plus the owner's acceptance authority at settlement. A unit whose checks pass but whose owner rejects is an audit event, recorded, and the check set is re-specified.

AttackEdit the test rather than fix the code.

DefenseFully mechanical, fully countered: the verification surface is itself under state comparison, byte-identical across the unit. Experiment E2 shows one such check converting 100% silent success into 100% detection.

AttackConvince the scorer to read the agent's summary.

DefenseRuled out by construction: the mint consumes only environment-captured state. Experiment E1 quantifies what re-admitting self-report costs: every false claim settles.

AttackSplit one outcome into many units to harvest divisible partial credit.

DefenseAtomic settlement as the default for outcomes whose value is holistic, and the owner's monopoly on unit creation: workers execute units; they do not define them.

The honest summary: quirq integrity reduces to environment integrity plus budget governance. The first is an engineering property, demonstrated. The second is an institutional property, stated as an open hypothesis, because that is what it is.

08Validation

Every hypothesis carries its falsifier.

The program is hypothesis-first: hypotheses stated at full strength, each bound to numbered experiments on an open harness whose results ledger is content-addressed. A skeptic re-runs and compares hashes rather than trusting the prose.

H1 · Mint integrity

State minting admits no fiction

False completion claims settle at rate zero when read from environment-captured state; any self-report channel settles them at the false-claim rate.

Falsifier · the environment arm settling any incomplete unit, or hardened checks failing to detect verification-surface attacks.

H2 · Ledger identification

Tenure lowers cost toward a floor

In persistent environments, cost per quirq falls with unit index toward a stable execution floor; fresh contexts pay cold start every time.

Falsifier · flat or rising persistent-arm trajectories, or equal decay in fresh contexts.

H3 · Predictive validity

QER* predicts real outcomes

Audit-corrected QER trend predicts renewal, expansion, and P&L attribution better than token spend, task counts, or benchmark scores.

Falsifier · QER* failing to outperform those baselines in instrumented deployments.

H4 · Budget governance

Inflation is bounded

Under payer-set budgets, benchmark anchoring, and sampled value audits, the ratio of budgets to audited value stays within tolerance under sustained pressure.

Falsifier · systematic budget drift despite the mitigations.
