Provenance. Every path, line number and excerpt was read from the pinned checkout. Numbers marked "measured here" come from git version 2.55.0 on a 2-vCPU Linux box (git's own CPU count reports 8), in the two-file lab repository, the six-object pack repository or a clone of git.git. Commit timings are medians of 21 runs; pack timings are single or paired runs that moved by up to 40% between sessions, so read them as ratios. The syscall shim sees only calls that go through the PLT and misses glibc-internal opens. The mechanisms marked "reconstruction" are mine. I did not verify why upstream's git.git pack deltifies 5.3% of its commits, did not measure pack-objects output with more than one thread, bitmap lookup tables or pseudo-merges, and did not trace a commit under the reftable backend. The path-walk comparison ran without --revs. Linus's 2017 comments on SHAttered are not quoted, because lore.kernel.org refused every request.
