The problem

AI profits from your consumption,
not your company's ROI.

Token sellers keep scaling the price of tokens while offering little insight into how they are used. The bill tells you what the machine consumed. It says nothing about what your business got back.

More tokens is not more work

Spend scales with consumption, not finished output. The meter runs whether or not the job gets done, and a retry loop costs the most of all.

You cannot see inside

No per-agent, per-task view of token, CPU, and infra spend. You see what the platforms want you to see, one pooled line item at a time.

Under the hood

Powered by the quirq: a unit of verified work.

Behind every number on the dashboard is one simple rule from our research. A task carries a budget set by the person who wants it done. The workspace snapshots the world before and after, verifies completion against a definition of done, and mints budget × completion as delivered value. Nothing is self-reported, so the ledger can be trusted.
