Reading the status column

"Trunk" means 09f2dc1320 (2026-11-06) where a verifier checked it, and trunk was read, never built or run. "Not checked" means nobody did, and it says nothing about whether the finding still holds. Absence from Jira is weaker still: D3's "No Jira matched two text searches" is not proof that none exists. "The verifier" is a separate pass that re-read code and reran scenarios against the research dossiers. Its corrections override them.

Limits of the evidence

Rows carry the labels of what stands behind them. Every timing is loopback on a shared 2-vCPU box: relative behaviour, never capacity. Hand-flipped bytes and truncate -s tail cuts are simulated damage, and any count they yield is set by the mock: 987 of 2,000 in D62 is never a loss rate, and no real power cut was run. Faults injected through a proxy are named, as in D4. Single runs are called single runs. librdkafka was read, never run, and its figures, as in D77, are computed.

Claims are condensed here; evidence and reproduction commands stay in the dossier. The verifiers sampled read-in-code facts and relied on a citation checker for quotes, so a fact not in their tables was not individually judged. Every row describes the pin.
