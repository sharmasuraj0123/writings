PostgreSQL

  • PostgreSQL 18 Released! (25 September 2025): asynchronous I/O, uuidv7(), virtual generated columns as the default.
  • PostgreSQL 19 Beta 1 Released! and PostgreSQL 19 Beta 4 Released! (24 September 2026): the 19 status and the features reverted before GA.
